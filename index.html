Features

Everything you need for fast, private communication

End-to-End Encryption

Direct messages are encrypted client-side with Curve25519 (X25519), HKDF-SHA256, and AES-256-GCM, plus 60-digit safety numbers and QR verification.

HD Audio & Video Calls

1-on-1 and multi-speaker WebRTC calls with screen sharing, shareable call links, and a floating mini-player for multitasking.

Rich Messaging & Voice Notes

Hands-free voice notes with animated waveforms and 1x/1.5x/2x playback, emoji reactions, message editing, polls, documents, location, and contacts.

Stories & Ephemeral Status

Share photo, video, and text updates that disappear after 24 hours, with privacy controls for who can view your stories.

Encrypted Cloud Backups

Back up and restore your chat history encrypted with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 passwords or a 64-character recovery key.

Regional Payments & Localization

Designed for the Democratic Republic of Congo and global users with multi-language support and integrated mobile money workflows.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Privacy by design, verified on your device

Your private keys are generated and stored on your device. Servers only route encrypted ciphertext, nonces, and authentication tags—never readable message plaintext.

Account & Data Control

Delete Your Masolo Account & Data

You can permanently delete your Masolo account and associated personal data directly inside the app or by submitting a deletion request if you no longer have access to the app.

Option 1: Delete Directly In-App (Immediate)

  1. Open the Masolo app on Android or iOS.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the account actions and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the prompt to permanently erase your account.

Option 2: Request Deletion Without the App

If you have uninstalled Masolo or cannot sign in, email support@masolo.app from your contact email with the subject Masolo Account Deletion Request and include the phone number registered to your account. We will verify ownership and process your request within 30 days.

What is deleted when you delete your account:
  • Your authentication account and phone-number user profile.
  • Your call history, payment activity records, chat state, and private account metadata.
  • Your published stories/statuses and their associated uploaded media files.

Retention note: Messages already delivered to other participants remain on their devices. Minimal server logs or records required for fraud prevention or legal compliance may be retained temporarily before automatic expiration.

Privacy Policy

Privacy & Data Handling

Masolo collects only the data required to operate messaging, calling, and security features:

  • Account info: Phone number, optional display name, and user ID for authentication and contact discovery.
  • Messages & media: Encrypted message payloads, voice notes, photos, videos, files, and optional shared locations.
  • Diagnostics & security: Crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics), push notification tokens (FCM), and app integrity tokens (App Check).

All data is encrypted in transit over TLS/DTLS. Masolo does not sell your personal data or share it with third-party advertisers.
